Shapes + Elevation — isolating why the cards aren't landing

Same 7-tool grid, same content, same locked palette and type — only radius, border, shadow, and padding change between the six versions below. The goal isn't to pick a winner yet; it's to find out which single variable (or combination) is actually the problem, since "not a fan of cards" has come up repeatedly even after the border was already trimmed to hairline-only.

How to react: for each version, name what specifically feels off (or right) — e.g. "V1 and V2 both feel too boxed in, V4 is closer" isolates radius/density as the issue rather than border. "Still don't like any of them" is also useful data — it may mean the grid itself, not the card, needs to change shape.
1Sharp, bordered — current baseline / control
What's been shown in every render so far, included here as the reference point everything else is judged against. Isolates nothing new — it's the known quantity.

Same 12px radius as V3, but both border and shadow are dropped entirely — the card reads as its own region purely through a slightly different fill tone (#EAEBED) against the page background (#F4F5F6). No edge treatment at all. Isolates: elevation cue itself — shadow vs. nothing, border vs. nothing.
