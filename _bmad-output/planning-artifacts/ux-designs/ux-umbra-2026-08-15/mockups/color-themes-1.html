Umbra — Color Themes (Round 1)

Five palette variations exploring a precision industrial accent orange (iPhone 17 Pro finish / Leica shutter-dot register) over a graduated neutral-gray hierarchy. Each block shows every semantic token as a light/dark swatch pair, then the same sidebar + tool-grid content rendered in both modes.
